## 1.4.0

- New slider "Ground bases landing": how often hives send relays to found ground bases on the planets of their star. Above ×1 a hive sends more than one per check and keeps more out; 0 stops new landings.
- New slider "Ground bases per planet": raises the game's limit of 7 relays per planet and hive, up to ×5.
- Both are stored per galaxy like the other factors and are part of the presets.
- Hints below the sliders follow their positions and say what else to change: spread and build speed for more landings, unit capacity and production for bigger fleets, settings that do nothing at Passive, values that stop landings or spread, frame rate at very high capacity, and new hive settings that only affect new hives.
- The panel grows to fit its rows and hints.

## 1.3.4

- New slider "Threat level": sets the attack meter of every hive and ground base at once; 100% starts their attacks. Passive levels still never attack.
- An aggressiveness value that is none of the game's six levels is no longer replaced by Dummy when another slider moves.
- Config values edited to 0 or below their minimum are read as the minimum.

## 1.3.3

- Close button hover no longer sticks out of its frame: the box is 48 wide so the frame's slanted end stays clear of the button.

## 1.3.2

- The close button sits in a box of its own size: the window no longer shows an empty slot left of the X.

## 1.3.1

- Fixed values and headings glowing in the new look.

## 1.3.0

- New look built from the game's own window colours and fonts: a deeper panel that stays readable on bright ground, glass buttons, a round slider thumb.
- Each slider now has a mark at the Normal value. A bar runs from that mark: orange when harder than Normal, blue when easier. Values at Normal are dimmed.
- The button of the preset you are on is highlighted.
- Rows are grouped, and difficulty and metadata stand out below them.

## 1.2.0

- Redesigned look: dark buttons, thin sliders with a warm accent, grouped rows.

## 1.1.0

- The panel now uses the game's own window, buttons and sliders.
- The panel is English only; the language setting is gone.
